Brachas with Rimon

Privacy Policy

Effective July 30, 2026

Brachas with Rimon is a study aid for learning the blessings said over food. We collect as little as possible, and nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.

What we collect

  • If you create an account (optional): your display name, email address, and a friend code, plus your practice progress (counts and streaks) so the friends league can work across devices.
  • If you photograph a meal: the photo is sent to our server, forwarded once to Anthropic's Claude API solely to identify the foods, and is not stored by us afterward.
  • If you enable reminders: your chosen reminder times, and (web only) a push subscription. In the iOS app, reminders are scheduled on your device and never leave it.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no sale of data — ever.
  • No passwords: your email plus your friend code signs you in.
  • Streaks and study progress live on your device unless you create an account.

Deleting your data

Open Account → delete my account permanently inside the app. This immediately and irreversibly removes your name, email, friend links, and synced progress from our server. You can also email us (below) and we will delete it for you.

Where data lives

Account data is stored on our server hosted by Railway (US). Meal photos pass through Anthropic's API under Anthropic's privacy policy and are not used to train models.

Children

The app is made for general audiences and collects no more from children than from anyone else. Accounts are optional; the full learning experience works without one.
