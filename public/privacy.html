Brachas with Rimon

Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026

Brachas with Rimon is a study aid for learning the blessings said over food. It runs as an iOS app and as a web app, and this policy covers both. We collect only what the features below need, and nothing is sold, shared for advertising, or used to track you.

What we collect

  • Your account. Sign-in is required because meal identification runs on our server and each account gets a daily allowance of it. We store your display name, your email address, and your password only as a scrypt hash (we cannot read it back). If you sign in with Apple or Google instead, we store the identifier that provider issues for you, plus your name and email if the provider shares them.
  • Your practice. Points, streaks, per-blessing counts, badges and a per-day history, synced to your account so the friends league works and a new device picks up where you left off.
  • Leaderboards and chat. Which boards you belong to and the messages you post in a board's chat. Messages are visible to every member of that board and are kept until the board is deleted or you delete your account. Reports and blocks you file are stored so we can act on them.
  • Notifications. In the iOS app, a device token from Apple Push Notification service so we can deliver chat and league notifications; it is cleared when you sign out. Mealtime reminders on iOS are scheduled on your device and never leave it. On the web, your chosen reminder times and a push subscription.
  • Meal photos. A photo you take or choose is sent to our server and forwarded once to Anthropic's API solely to identify the foods on the plate. We do not store the photo afterward.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs, no sale of data — ever.
  • We never show your email to other users; friends and board members see only your display name, your scores, and the chat messages you choose to post.
  • We do not use your photos, messages, or progress to train any model.

Deleting your data

Open Account → delete my account permanently inside the app. This immediately and irreversibly removes everything tied to your account from our server: your name, email, password hash, sign-in identifiers, progress, board memberships, chat messages, reports, blocks, and any push token or subscription. A board you own passes to another member so the group keeps its history. You can also email us (below) and we will delete it for you.

Where data lives

Account data is stored on our server hosted by Railway (US); any backup copies are encrypted. Meal photos pass through Anthropic's API under Anthropic's privacy policy and are not used to train models.

To display the app we also load fonts from Google Fonts, and the daily parsha reading comes from Hebcal and Sefaria (the date and Torah reference only). Like any web request, these providers receive your IP address and browser details — never your name, email, or study data.

Children

The app is made for general audiences and collects no more from children than from anyone else.
