Brachas with Rimon

Community rules & terms of use

Effective September 22, 2026 · Developer: Shanan Cohen, Group App LLC

Brachas with Rimon is a study aid for learning the blessings said over food. Most of it is just you and Rimon. The social features — friends, leaderboards and board chat — put you in touch with other people, so they come with a few rules. By creating an account or posting in a board chat you agree to them.

Board chat rules

  • Be kind. No harassment, bullying, threats, hate speech or slurs, and nothing sexual or graphic. Disagree about brachos all you like; attack ideas, not people.
  • Stay on topic. No spam, advertising, scams, links to malware, or repeated unwanted messages.
  • Respect privacy. Don't post other people's personal information (phone numbers, addresses, private messages) or pretend to be someone you are not.
  • Keep it legal. Nothing that infringes a copyright or breaks the law.
  • Halacha is learned, not decided here. Chat is for encouragement and questions, not rulings. For any practical question, ask a qualified rabbi.

How moderation works

  • A board chat is only visible to people who hold that board's code — it is never a public room. You can leave a board at any time.
  • Every message from someone else has Report and Block. Blocking hides that person's messages from you on every board immediately. Reports go to the app's team, who review them — usually within a day — and may delete messages, remove a member from a board, or close an account.
  • A word filter refuses obvious slurs at send time.
  • Repeated or serious violations end an account. We may act on our own initiative, not only on reports.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you write. You grant us the right to store and show your messages to the members of that board, and to remove them to enforce these rules. Deleting your account deletes every message you posted (see the privacy policy and how to delete your account).

The app itself

  • The app is provided as is, free of charge, as a learning aid. Blessing guidance is drawn from published sources (chabad.org, brachos.org, OU Kosher) and photo identification can be wrong. For any practical halachic question, consult a qualified rabbi.
  • We may change or discontinue features, and update these terms; the effective date above changes when we do.
  • You must be at least 13 to create an account.
